Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, email address and password. Your password is stored in a protected form that we cannot read.
  • Order and delivery details: your name, phone number, delivery address, PIN code, the products you order, order value, delivery distance and order status.
  • Location: at checkout we ask your browser for your current location. See section 4.
  • Payment information: you pay by UPI, directly to our UPI ID, using your own UPI app. We do not receive your UPI PIN or bank login details. We store the 12-digit UTR (transaction reference) that you enter on the website, so that we can match your payment to your order, and the payment status of the order. If you send us a payment screenshot or message on WhatsApp, we also receive that screenshot and your WhatsApp number.
  • Agent referral information: if you choose an agent when creating your account, we record that choice on your account and with your orders. If you apply to become an agent, we collect your name, phone number, username and password.
  • Messages: emails or messages you send to us, and your conversations with customer care.
  • Technical information: details such as your IP address and browser type, collected automatically when you use the website.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account, including sending password-reset emails;
  • process, deliver and support your orders, and calculate delivery distance and charges;
  • check your UPI payment against our bank records and process refunds;
  • contact you about your orders, complaints and support requests;
  • calculate and pay commission to agents for referred orders;
  • keep the website secure and prevent fraud and misuse; and
  • meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing messages unless you agree to receive them.

By creating an account or placing an order you consent to the uses described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time, but then we may not be able to continue your account or complete pending orders.

4. Location information

To place a delivery order, checkout asks your browser to detect your location. We use it only to calculate your distance from our store and any delivery charge. We store the calculated distance with your order. We do not store your exact location coordinates. You control location sharing through your browser or device settings, but we cannot calculate delivery without it.

5. Who we share information with

We share only what is needed for the purpose:

  • Delivery: our delivery staff receive your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • Your bank and UPI app: you make the payment through your own UPI app and bank, under their terms and privacy policies.
  • WhatsApp: if you send us a payment screenshot or message on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy also applies.
  • Email service provider (Resend): to send emails such as password-reset emails.
  • Hosting and storage providers (Render for the website and Cloudinary for product images).
  • Font and icon services (Google Fonts and jsDelivr): when a page loads, your browser connects to these services, so they may receive your IP address and browser details.
  • Finance provider: if you choose to apply for Bajaj Finance through us, information for that application is handled by Bajaj Finance under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Agents: we record which agent you selected so that commission can be calculated. An agent can see the order number, the customer name, the order status and the commission for orders referred through them, but not your phone number or delivery address.
  • Authorities: where the law requires us to.

These providers handle information under their own terms. Some of them may store or process information on servers outside India.

6. Cookies

We use only the cookies the website needs to work. A session cookie keeps you logged in and remembers your cart, and a security cookie protects our forms. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

You can block cookies in your browser settings, but login, cart and checkout may then not work.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is active. We keep order, payment and delivery records for as long as needed to support you, handle disputes and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. After that, we delete or anonymise the information.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, protected password storage and limited access to your information. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and tell us at once if you suspect misuse of your account.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update inaccurate information;
  • delete your information, subject to records we must keep by law; and
  • stop using your information on the basis of your consent.

To make a request, email us from your registered email address. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach any authority competent under applicable law.

10. Children

This website is not meant for children under 18. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian. If we learn that we collected a child’s information without parent or guardian consent, we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when necessary. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page. Any changes are subject to applicable law and do not remove rights that have already accrued.
